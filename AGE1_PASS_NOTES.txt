March of Epochs React v0.17.0 — Age I quality pass

This pass focuses on making Campaign 1 / Age I feel end-to-end playable and more polished.

Included highlights:
- Always-mobile / vertical-first layout, including on desktop and laptop.
- Battle screen stacked in a mobile-style layout instead of a broken desktop split view.
- Command selection now remains paused until the player chooses a target or cancels.
- Larger battlefield armies, improved spacing, and numbered target markers to reduce overlap/confusion.
- Better target cards for Rally / Focus / Reinforce.
- Campaign 1 encounter rebalance so a fresh account can progress through Age I with learning/progression.
- Starter onboarding boost: initial Gold / EXP and 2 starter artifacts.
- Added 2 new early artifacts: Stone Axe Grip and Bone Charm.
- Updated artifact pack pools to include the new early artifacts.
- Mobile-width UI polish across campaign, artifacts, armies, loadouts, and achievements.
- Local storage key bumped to v017 to start from a clean save state for this pass.
