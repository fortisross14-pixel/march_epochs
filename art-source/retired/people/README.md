# Retired People artwork

These are the original Age I serious painted portraits/cards and Age I/II vector placeholders. They are preserved for comparison and rollback, outside the runtime asset tree. Do not import them into the game or use them as style anchors for new People art.

Active replacements and retained approved People are in `src/assets/ages/<ageId>/people/`. See `docs/PEOPLE-ART-AND-COLLECTIONS.md` for the complete audit.
